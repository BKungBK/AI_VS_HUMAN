# Creative refinement · 6 October 2026

User feedback: preserve the successful camera journey; improve the gavel's material, form and strike loop; give the content more interesting idle motion; remove background clutter; replace the unpleasant scene-travel sound. The requested three background treatments and varied loop intensity are selected per cue, rather than exposed as viewer preferences. Full motion remains identical across OS preferences.

## Diagnosis

The browser audit found 17 of 31 content cues with a static foreground after entrance. The other 14 had foreground motion, but several consisted only of a small pulse. Moving walls alone do not make a diagram tell a story. The checkpoint repeats rectangular outlines over the reading area; the forum repeats full-height columns behind the headline. The ECG overlaps the verification card label. The gavel is a flat outlined symbol without a contact surface, material shading or a strike cycle.

Travel sound uses FFT noise plus rising/falling pitched components, followed by a destination tone and sometimes another glass tone. This identifies a candidate source of the synthetic character; it is not a claim of a listening review. User feedback specifically targets scene travel. Adjust departure and travel first; preserve mute, volume, speech-space processing and the shared clock.

## Direction

Keep the dark slate, coral, periwinkle, mint/gold and local Thai typography. Use quiet reading regions surrounded by a few broad material surfaces, peripheral silhouettes, soft light and atmosphere. During travel, those surfaces become visible enough to establish scale and position. As the camera lands, the same architecture becomes quieter. Avoid a tunnel of nested outlines.

Assign quiet backgrounds to evidence, legal facts and process panels; balanced backgrounds to the opening, mind comparison and listening scenes; more visible environments to art, the frontier, grove and horizon. Even the immersive treatment protects headline and card reading regions. Camera paths, departure continuity and text entry remain.

Each content cue gets one dominant visual behavior. Continuous motion suits token flow and ECG; events with a rest suit the gavel, network propagation and verification; slow material/light motion suits photographs, empathy evidence and the closing. Text, exact values, photographs and scientific claims remain readable and stable. Graphics are illustrations, not live calculations or changing measurements. Game/leaderboard/award placeholders remain centered text.

The SVG gavel has a shaded walnut head and handle, metal collars, contact shadow and a solid sound block. Its loop has anticipation, fast downward acceleration, contact, small rebound and a long rest. The camera and card do not shake with every strike. Repeated visual loops do not add repeated audible hits beneath the live speaker.

Travel audio becomes a restrained, smooth, non-tonal passage of air with a low-level material texture. Remove pitch glides and threshold bells from the travel stems, soften the high band, reduce travel gain and stereo sweep. The arrival remains a short cue rather than a continuing bed. Auditory preference remains a human listening check; waveform/timing tests cover technical behavior only.
