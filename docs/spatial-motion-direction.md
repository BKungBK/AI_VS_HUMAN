# Spatial transitions — revision 3

Purpose: give the seminar a continuous spatial world. One PerspectiveCamera drives cards, HTML typography, far background planes and particles. The projector view stays readable after each landing.

| Phase | Timing | Attention and movement |
|---|---:|---|
| Departure | 0–0.48s | Old headline lines roll and travel in Z; the old card group peels away. Camera starts at the actual previous pose. |
| Transit | 0.08–1.60s | Camera pulls into a wide view, then makes a motivated push, arc or dolly. Near typography and far planes show different parallax. |
| Arrival | 0.56–2.30s | New title lines turn toward the viewer with a small stagger; subtitle follows. Cards and text share the camera. |
| Read | from 2.40s | Camera becomes frontal at Z1500, FOV39.60°. Text transforms return to identity; ambient narrative loops remain subtle. |

Families: punch for evidence/statistics, lateral arc for a change of viewpoint, push-through for a new section, compensated Z/FOV dolly for frontier and confidence. The dolly aims back toward the focus plane while FOV narrows, preserving the safe area for incoming Thai lines. Backward navigation reverses the travel direction. Transitions into game/leaderboard/award placeholders use a short fade and plain centered text.

Text remains native HTML, shaped as complete Thai lines. No glyph splitting, bitmap text, extruded meshes, or perpetual text drift. Exit and entry are separated so two headlines do not compete. Header/footer stay in screen space as navigation anchors.

Background: three far CSS3D planes at Z−900/−1800/−2900, slate with restrained coral/periwinkle light, broad frames and orbital contours. Light blends between human, AI and shared cues over 1.05s. These add scale and depth without replacing the approved theme. Same clock and seeded particles. Camera and text always use the full motion sequence regardless of system motion preferences. CSS3D retains full content when WebGL is absent.

Audio: cinematic-motion-studio and hyperframes-animation guide the visual choreography. hyperframes-audio was inspected; no placed audio tracks exist to mix. This revision remains silent, consistent with the approved live seminar plan.
